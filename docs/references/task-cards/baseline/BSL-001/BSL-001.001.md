## Outcome
Initial baseline established from 30 days of resolver logs.

- Median outbound query rate per host: ~410/day; p95: ~2,900/day.
- Record types: A/AAAA 91%, PTR 4%, TXT 3%, other 2%.
- Sanctioned resolvers only; no direct-to-Internet port 53 egress observed.
- Two known benign outliers recorded: the monitoring collector (high PTR
  volume) and the CI runner pool (bursty A/AAAA lookups during builds).
