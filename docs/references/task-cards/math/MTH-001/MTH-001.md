# MTH-001 - Monthly authentication peer-group anomaly review

## Why
Global thresholds hide unusual authentication behavior by both noisy service
accounts and low-volume users. This recurring task groups accounts by role and
activity level, recalculates robust peer baselines, and reviews the strongest
outliers for compromised credentials or previously unknown automation.
